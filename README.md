                      Visualization 1
This was an introduction to Power BI
I learnt how to get data from excel, transform it with some little editing before it is transformed to Power BI.Apparently power BI keeps a record of every step I take in editting the data.
For this case I was working on Apocalypse Food Prep to answer these questions: 1.Where are we buying the same products using the least amount of money?
                                                                                  To help me determine where to buy different products.
                                                                               2.Should I buy all the products at the same place or are there products that are going to be expensive and cheap in other places where i should get them?
The data contained columns for stores, products,prices and the date they were purchased.
From the visualisations I created, Costo is the place to go for cheap products except rice if you want to safe a few dollars you can get rice at Target.
